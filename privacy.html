Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Who we are

Echo of the Wine is an independent wine study and tasting app made by Isaac Benítez. Contact: echoofthewine@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no cloud service. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

What stays on your device

Your tasting notes, conclusions, reveals, reflections, study answers, confidence choices, review dates and practice marks are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. They may be included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple manages under your own account settings.

Backups and sharing you choose

You can export a backup file or share tasting notes using iPhone’s standard share and Files features. You choose where they go; the service you choose applies its own privacy practices. Exported files are not encrypted by the app.

Links to sources

Questions link to their sources. Opening a link takes you to that website, which receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address and applies its own privacy policy. The app does not send your notes with these links.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. (Offloading the app may keep its data.) We cannot see, retrieve or delete data on your device because we never receive it.

Email

If you write to us, we use your message only to answer it, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send passwords or private information.

Children

The app is about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
