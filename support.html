Protect your notes

Your notes live only on your iPhone. Before changing phones or deleting the app, open Settings → Export backup to Files and keep the file somewhere safe.

Restore a backup

Open Settings → Import native backup and choose the file. Missing flights and study records are added; records you already have are kept as they are.

How review works

A missed question comes back after 10 minutes; a correct but unsure answer after one day; confident correct answers come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days. The app does not send notifications.

Report a wrong answer or source

Email the question, what you believe is wrong, and an official reference if you have one. Questions are reviewed and corrected in app updates.

Possible matches in tasting

After you lock a wine, the app compares your notes with study profiles of classic wines. It shows tendencies to learn from, not an identification of the wine in your glass.

Offline use

Study content and tasting notes work without internet. Source links need a connection.
